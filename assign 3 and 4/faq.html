Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find detailed answers regarding rules, team registrations, travel allowances, campus access, and certificates.

General & Registration Queries

1. Is there an entry registration fee for INNOVIZ 2026?

No, registration for all technical hackathons, quizzes, and workshops is completely free. Only RoboWars and Battle of the Bands require a nominal team security deposit of ₹300, which is 100% refundable upon reporting.

2. What items must I bring for verification at the registration desk?

All team members must carry their original college/university ID cards and a printed or digital copy of the registration confirmation email with QR code.

3. Can team members be from different colleges?

Yes! Cross-college and cross-departmental teams are allowed for CodeQuest Hackathon and WebCraft design sprints. However, for Esports and RoboWars, all members must represent the same registered university.

4. How will cash prizes and awards be disbursed?

Cash awards and certificates will be distributed during the Grand Valedictory Ceremony on Day 3 (October 26). Prize money will be transferred directly to the team leader's verified bank account within 3 working days.

5. Who do I contact if I face technical issues while submitting the form?

You can drop an email with a screenshot of the error to innoviz2026@university.edu or visit our Contact & Support Page for direct helpline numbers.
