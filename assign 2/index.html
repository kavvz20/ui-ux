Essential Travel Tips

Helpful advice to make your journey smooth and stress-free:

  • Book in Advance: Book flights and hotels 6 to 8 weeks early for cheaper rates.
  • Keep Digital Copies: Save photos of your passport, visa, and tickets on your phone.
  • Pack Smart: Check the weather forecast before packing and travel light.
  • Carry Local Currency: Keep some cash handy for local street markets and tips.
  • Get Travel Insurance: Protect your vacation against unexpected delays or emergencies.
